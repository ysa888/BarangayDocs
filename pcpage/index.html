Our History

History of Barangay New Banicain

From a fishing village across the bay to a thriving residential community in the heart of Olongapo City.

Pre-warFishing and small-scale farming were the main livelihoods.
1948The U.S. Naval Base expansion led to the relocation of the barrio.
TodayA residential community beside West Tapinac, Olongapo City.
Pre-war Days

Old Barrio Banicain

Old Barrio Banicain was formerly situated in an area across the sea overlooking the U.S. Navy yard. During the pre-war days, fishing and minute farming were the main sources and means of livelihood of the residents in the barrio. The villagers regularly came to the town of Olongapo in their sailboats to bring their products of firewood, cashew nuts and bananas. Out of their sales they bought pork, beef and other foodstuffs, clothes and shoes to take back home to Barrio Banicain.

Circa 1948

Relocation to West Tapinac

When the U.S. Naval base was expanded sometime in 1948, Barrio Banicain was affected and relocated along the western portion of Barrio West Tapinac. To assist the displaced residents of the barrio, quonset huts were constructed by the U.S. Navy where the residents were temporarily housed. Soon however, the land comprising the barrio was opened for occupancy and those already residing in the place were given preferential right to occupy the area where they had established their homes.

Rebuilding

A New Residential Community

The new site was formerly low and swampy, but when the U.S. Naval base officials embarked on a continuous rehabilitation program in Olongapo, the entire place was refilled and elevated, transforming the barrio into a suitable residential place. Soon, several employees working inside the U.S. Naval Base preferred to settle in the barrio and built permanent residential houses in the area.

By registering an account on BarangayDocs, you confirm that all information you provide is accurate, truthful, and complete. False information may result in the rejection or revocation of your account.

2. Purpose of the System

BarangayDocs is an official digital document request system of Barangay Banicain, Olongapo City. It is intended solely for registered residents of the barangay to request official barangay documents such as Barangay Clearance, Certificate of Residency, and others.

3. Account Approval

All new accounts are subject to review and approval by the Barangay Admin. You will be notified once your account has been verified and activated. Accounts that cannot be verified may be rejected.

5. Changes to Terms

Barangay Banicain reserves the right to update these Terms and Conditions at any time. Continued use of the system constitutes acceptance of any revised terms.

Privacy Policy

1. Data We Collect

We collect personal information including your full name, date of birth, sex, civil status, home address, years of residency, contact number, and email address. This information is required to verify your identity as a resident of Barangay Banicain.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively for processing barangay document requests, verifying your residency, and communicating updates regarding your requests. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your information is securely stored using Firebase, a cloud platform by Google, with access restricted to authorized Barangay personnel only. All data is handled in accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (R.A. 10173).

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact the Barangay Banicain office directly.

5. Cookies

This system may use session data to keep you logged in during your visit. No tracking cookies or third-party advertisements are used.
