About BarangayDocs

What is BarangayDocs?

BarangayDocs is an official digital document request system developed for Barangay Banicain, Olongapo City. It modernizes the process of requesting barangay documents, allowing residents to submit requests online without visiting the office.

Features

Submit requests for Barangay Clearance, Certificate of Residency, Certificate of Indigency, and more. Track your request status in real time — from submission to ready for pickup. Every document carries a unique QR code for instant authenticity verification.

Terms and Conditions

1. Acceptance of Terms

By registering an account on BarangayDocs, you confirm that all information you provide is accurate, truthful, and complete. False information may result in the rejection or revocation of your account.

2. Purpose of the System

BarangayDocs is an official digital document request system of Barangay Banicain, Olongapo City. It is intended solely for registered residents of the barangay to request official barangay documents such as Barangay Clearance, Certificate of Residency, and others.

3. Account Approval

All new accounts are subject to review and approval by the Barangay Admin. You will be notified once your account has been verified and activated. Accounts that cannot be verified may be rejected.

4. Proper Use

You agree to use this system only for lawful purposes. Submitting fraudulent document requests, impersonating another resident, or attempting to tamper with the system is strictly prohibited and may be subject to legal action.

5. Changes to Terms

Barangay Banicain reserves the right to update these Terms and Conditions at any time. Continued use of the system constitutes acceptance of any revised terms.

Privacy Policy

1. Data We Collect

We collect personal information including your full name, date of birth, sex, civil status, home address, years of residency, contact number, and email address. This information is required to verify your identity as a resident of Barangay Banicain.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively for processing barangay document requests, verifying your residency, and communicating updates regarding your requests. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your information is securely stored using Firebase, a cloud platform by Google, with access restricted to authorized Barangay personnel only. All data is handled in accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (R.A. 10173).

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact the Barangay Banicain office directly.

5. Cookies

This system may use session data to keep you logged in during your visit. No tracking cookies or third-party advertisements are used.
