How It Works

Simple. Fast. Paperless.

Getting your official barangay documents has never been easier. Follow these three steps and you're done.

1

Register Your Account

Sign up as a Barangay Banicain resident in minutes. Provide your personal details, address, and valid ID for verification. Account approval by our admin ensures security.

2

Submit Your Request

Browse available document types, fill out a short request form, and submit online — no need to visit the barangay hall just to file your request.

3

Pick Up Your Document

Track your request in real-time. Once processed, you'll be notified to pick up your QR-verified document at the barangay hall — fast and hassle-free.

Available Documents

Official Barangay Documents

Request any of the following documents online. All documents are official, signed, and QR-code verified by Barangay Banicain.

Barangay Clearance

A general clearance certifying that you have no pending case or derogatory record in the barangay. Commonly required for employment and government transactions.

Most Requested

Certificate of Residency

Certifies that you are a bona fide resident of Barangay Banicain, Olongapo City. Required for school enrollment, loan applications, and other official purposes.

Available Online

Certificate of Indigency

Certifies that you belong to a low-income family. Required for medical assistance, scholarship applications, and government welfare programs.

Available Online

Business Clearance

A permit certifying that your business is known and has no objection from the barangay. Required as part of business permit applications with the city.

Available Online

Solo Parent Certificate

Issued to qualified solo parents in the barangay to avail of benefits under Republic Act 8972 (Solo Parents' Welfare Act).

Available Online

Other Certifications

Additional barangay certifications and endorsements as may be required for specific official or legal purposes. Contact the barangay office for more details.

Terms and Conditions

1. Acceptance of Terms

By registering an account on BarangayDocs, you confirm that all information you provide is accurate, truthful, and complete. False information may result in the rejection or revocation of your account.

2. Purpose of the System

BarangayDocs is an official digital document request system of Barangay Banicain, Olongapo City. It is intended solely for registered residents of the barangay to request official barangay documents such as Barangay Clearance, Certificate of Residency, and others.

3. Account Approval

All new accounts are subject to review and approval by the Barangay Admin. You will be notified once your account has been verified and activated. Accounts that cannot be verified may be rejected.

4. Proper Use

You agree to use this system only for lawful purposes. Submitting fraudulent document requests, impersonating another resident, or attempting to tamper with the system is strictly prohibited and may be subject to legal action.

5. Changes to Terms

Barangay Banicain reserves the right to update these Terms and Conditions at any time. Continued use of the system constitutes acceptance of any revised terms.

Privacy Policy

1. Data We Collect

We collect personal information including your full name, date of birth, sex, civil status, home address, years of residency, contact number, and email address. This information is required to verify your identity as a resident of Barangay Banicain.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively for processing barangay document requests, verifying your residency, and communicating updates regarding your requests. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your information is securely stored using Firebase, a cloud platform by Google, with access restricted to authorized Barangay personnel only. All data is handled in accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (R.A. 10173).

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact the Barangay Banicain office directly.

5. Cookies

This system may use session data to keep you logged in during your visit. No tracking cookies or third-party advertisements are used.
